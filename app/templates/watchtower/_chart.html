{# Shared chart macros for the Watchtower dashboards. Every chart on these pages used to be its own hand-written SVG: no axis, no scale, no labels, and nothing to hover. These macros render the geometry `app/charts.py` computes, so all of them read the same way — a labelled y-axis with gridlines, dated x-axis ticks, a crosshair tooltip listing every series at the pointer, and a table view underneath for anyone who can't hover or can't separate the colours. The SVG deliberately does NOT set preserveAspectRatio="none". These charts carry text, and a non-uniform scale stretches the glyphs with the plot — the reason the old charts had to stay wordless. Styling lives in app.css (`.wtc-*`), the hover layer in app.js.
